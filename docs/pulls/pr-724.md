### #691 — TypedDbAuthority (`cad_logarithmic_quantity.py`)

Every logarithmic quantity is a sealed `CadLogQuantity` (logqty-): `quantity_class` (`absolute_log_level` / `relative_gain_loss` / `device_relative_setting` / `linear_value`), `domain` × `quantity` (acoustic SPL/SWL/Leq/band …, digital dBFS variants, electrical dBu/dBV, dimensionless gain/TL/IL), and a mandatory `CadLogReference` bundling value+unit+standard profile+weighting+band (`CadBandSpec`)+time integration — "74 dB" alone is physically undefined and is rejected. `RatioBasis` keeps 20log vs 10log a property of the physics, never the UI string. Cross-domain motion happens only through `CadCalibrationBridge` (logbrg-): a versioned, sha-pinned relation with `active`/`superseded`/`withdrawn` states — "0 dBFS = full-scale V_rms" is evidence, not a unit conversion.

`evaluate_log_operation` (sealed `CadLogOperation`, logop-) is fail-closed: `level_difference`/`compare` require identical reference identity else `incompatible_quantities`; `apply_gain` requires a relative/dimensionless gain, an absolute target, and a declared linear chain — limiter/compressor/driver-compression chains return `nonlinear_chain`, undeclared returns `unverified`; `energetic_sum` computes `10·log10(Σ10^(Li/10))` in the linear domain and refuses coherent sums without phase data (`coherent_requires_phase_data`); `arithmetic_mean` requires a declared `mean_method`; `convert` without a matching active bridge returns `requires_calibration_bridge`.

### #689 — IdentifiabilityAuthority (`cad_parameter_identifiability.py`)

`CadCalibrationParameter` (calprm-) seals role taxonomy (geometry/source/receiver/boundary/environment/device/nuisance-registration/discrepancy) and provenance: `directly_measured`/`manufacturer_lab_evidence` pin evidence refs; `calibration_adjusted`/`inverse_estimated`/`posterior_profile_estimate` pin the calibration run — adjusted and measured never mix in one store. `CadSensitivityEvidence` (idsens-) mandates method + parameter scaling + observable normalization + perturbation detail so numbers across methods are never compared raw. `CadParameterCorrelationEvidence` (idcorr-) carries mandatory assumptions; `CadEquivalentSolutionSet` (ideqset-) retains near-optimal members with `physically_distinct_claims`.

`evaluate_identifiability` (sealed `CadIdentifiabilityAssessment`, idassess-) ladder: distinct equivalent solutions → `structurally_non_unique`/`parameter_not_identifiable`; confirmed model-form discrepancy → `parameter_model_dependent`; measured/lab provenance → `parameter_identified_within_domain` with mandatory domain note; informative/assumed priors → `prior_dominated`; bound-saturated → `bound_dominated`; zero evidence → `insufficient_evidence`; insensitive entries or strong correlation/collinearity (|r|≥0.9 or qualitative `strong`) → `weakly_identifiable`; an unevaluated model-form question caps at `parameter_weakly_identified`; clean evidence earns only `parameter_identified_within_domain` — a global identifiability claim is structurally impossible. `evidence_needs` names the next discriminating experiment; nuisance-parameter correlation appends the "timing/position offset masquerading as material" caution.

### #698 — BenchmarkLeakageAuthority (`cad_validation_statistics.py`)

`CadValidationStatisticalDesign` (vsdes-) seals the `generalization_claim`, the `independent_unit` (room/config/source/receiver/pair/session/installation — frequency bins and repeats are not expressible as units), an explicit `CadHierarchyLevel` nest, and separated raw/independent/effective counts (effective size requires its estimation method). `independent_cap()` computes the defensible ceiling from the hierarchy. `CadDependenceModel` (vsdep-) declares dependence classes, spatial-correlation model (Kuster reverberant family / measured covariance / …), frequency-bin coupling causes, and the bootstrap resampling unit. `CadDatasetRoleAssignment` (vsrole-) pins each corpus's epistemic role; `CadBenchmarkExposureRecord` (vsexp-) is the append-only ledger of what was revealed to which solver version and what decision followed.

`evaluate_validation_claim` (sealed `CadChallengeQualification`, vsqual-): undeclared independent counts → `insufficient_evidence`; counts above the hierarchy cap → `pseudoreplication_detected`; split granularity below claim granularity → `split_mismatch`; selection-exposed claim corpus → `winner_selection_biased`; development/fit-exposed → `development_validation_only`; spatial/frequency structure without a dependence model → `independence_unestablished`; untouched locked-challenge corpus → `locked_challenge_eligible`; residual unknowns → `qualified_with_limitations`; else `qualified_generalization`.

### Literature basis

- **Identifiability**: Mondet et al. 2024 (Bayesian calibration fits despite unidentifiable params); Wulbusch et al. 2024 (model-form discrepancy and physical parameters); Raue et al. (profile likelihood; structural vs practical identifiability); Morris & Sobol sensitivity analysis; ill-posed inverse problems admit multiple optima — good fit ≠ unique physical parameters.
- **Benchmark leakage**: Dwork et al. 2015, *The reusable holdout* (adaptive re-use degrades holdouts → exposure ledger); Hurlbert 1984 pseudoreplication (correlated observations are not independent units); Kuster 2008 (reverberant-field spatial correlation — a different seat is not independence); Johnson & Long 1999 (smoothed-spectrum bin dependence).
- **dB typing**: ISO 80000-8:2020 + Amd 1:2025 (reference/weighting/time-integration are part of quantity identity); AES17-2020 (dBFS conventions); IEC 61672 / IEC 61260; SMPTE RP 200 (−20 dBFS → 85 dBC cinema chain as a typed bridge).
