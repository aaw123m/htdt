## Summary

Implements the three measurement-evidence reliability authorities from the REV56 wave:

- **#572 (P0)** `MeasurementUncertaintyBudget` — a sealed (`mub:<sha>`) uncertainty budget bound to measurement evidence. Contributors carry a kind (16) AND a provenance class (`REPEATABILITY`/`CALIBRATION_CERTIFICATE`/`MANUFACTURER_SPEC`/`STANDARD_TYPICAL_VALUE`/`GEOMETRY_TOLERANCE`/`MODEL_PROPAGATION`/`ASSUMED_BOUND`/`UNKNOWN`); validators make an assumed bound incapable of posing as calibration evidence and require GUM Type A empirical samples / Type B evidence refs. Combination via `propagate_uncertainty_budget` (#979): RSS with declared correlation groups, bounded worst-case, declared-only (no fabricated combination), plus a GUM-S1-style Monte-Carlo path with declared algorithm/seed/count. Frequency/level-varying budgets combine only on identical declared grids. `CalibrationChainLink` records the chain instrument→calibrator→reference→SI with `linked/broken/unknown` states — a broken or unknown link makes `traceable_documented` unclaimable, and `relative_only` cannot back an absolute-SPL measurand. `CalibratorCheck` pre/post pairs feed `evaluate_calibrator_drift`. `classify_residual`/`assess_residual_report`/`evaluate_delta_significance` produce the four honest verdicts (`RESIDUAL_CLEARLY_ABOVE_/COMPARABLE_TO_/BELOW_RESOLUTION_OF_/INSUFFICIENT_UNCERTAINTY_INFORMATION` and the delta equivalents) against REV55 residual reports — a 0.2 dB improvement stays `delta_inconclusive` when combined evidence uncertainty is larger.
- **#573 (P0)** `MeasurementStateSnapshot`/`StateControlPolicy`/`StateComparabilityVerdict` — sealed (`mss:`/`spol:`/`msv:`) capture-time state evidence and the fail-closed gate. Every observed attribute is explicit-or-UNKNOWN; `evaluate_state_comparability` (precedence `state_changed > insufficient_state_evidence > comparable_with_declared_drift > comparable_stable_state`) invalidates phase/timing/decay on environment drift before magnitude, treats engaged dynamic DSP or observed limiter engagement as contamination, and returns `insufficient_state_evidence` whenever required evidence is missing or UNKNOWN — never a universal threshold; all tolerances live in the versioned policy. `evaluate_sequence_stationarity` gates bounded repeat sequences on per-observable policy tolerances.
- **#575 (P1)** `MeasurementTransform` — the sealed (`mtr:<sha>`) immutable DAG of measurement transforms (21 kinds). `TransformInput`s pin measured datasets/IRs/upstream transforms by sha256; `output_capabilities ⊆ ⋂input_capabilities ∩ allowed-by-kind` is validator-enforced (downgrade-only). Averaging classes (`repeat_capture`/`spatial`/`operator_blend`/`declared_purpose`) stay distinct — spatial averages require distinct positions and alignment for phase/physical claims, repeat averages reject mixed positions. `AlignmentSpec` distinguishes measured common timing references (loopback/acoustic/known-hardware) from estimated cross-correlation (algorithm/band declared) and manual/direct-arrival/unknown — estimated alignment can never output `absolute_timing_valid`. `ClockCorrectionSpec` requires `raw_source_preserved=True`. Arithmetic validates declared quantity-domain pairs (dB×dB multiply is refused). `apply_transform` is an honest FR-domain executor: it verifies input identities, refuses silent regridding, implements rms/db/vector/hybrid averages, level alignment (declared span), trace arithmetic, normalize/smooth/band-aggregate/resample/frequency-merge, and refuses time-domain kinds rather than faking DSP. `DerivedFrequencyResponseOutput` (content-sealed) carries the intersection of capability flags so derived evidence is never indistinguishable from measured.
- **Persistence/integration**: native schema v21→v22 adds six canonical tables (budgets, significance assessments, state policies, snapshots, verdicts, transforms) with `native_row_integrity` bindings and `native_authority_audit` replay probes; `CadMeasurementEvidenceRepository` is append-only with idempotent saves and conflict errors; quality-page detail lines show the uncertainty summary/traceability/state verdict (honest 未登録/未記録 when absent). REV55 `PredictionMeasurementResidualReport` observables are assessed directly — no parallel stores.

## Literature basis

- ISO/IEC Guide 98-3 (GUM): `u_c(y)=sqrt(Σu_i²)` for uncorrelated inputs, declared correlation groups summed linearly, `U=k·u_c` (k≈2≈95%), Type A (empirical samples) vs Type B (certificate/spec/bounds) evaluation, GUM-S1 Monte-Carlo with declared distribution assignments.
- JCGM metrological-traceability vocabulary: chain links recorded with state; a chain ending without a `reference_standard`/`si_traceable_reference`, or containing a `broken`/`unknown` link, cannot support a `traceable_documented` claim.
- REW measurement semantics: RMS (incoherent power) vs dB vs vector/complex averaging, SPL alignment over a declared span, measured vs estimated timing references, clock-drift correction preserving the raw source.
- Prawda & Schlecht & Välimäki (2024): room impulse responses are non-stationary under temperature drift — phase/arrival time degrade first, which is why environment drift invalidates `phase_comparable`/`timing_comparable`/`decay_comparable` before `magnitude_comparable`; Cramer (1993) for the temperature→sound-speed bound.
