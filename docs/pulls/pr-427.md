## Summary

REV23-MACHINE — generative state-machine testing round. Adds `backend/tests/test_round23_state_machine.py`: seeded `random.Random` walks (530 cases) over five state-machine surfaces asserting invariants scripted tests can't reach — journal integrity under bounded history, persisted round-trips, `is_dirty`/dirty-state truth, worker lifecycle attribution, and list totals. Nine real invariant violations surfaced by the walks and fixed in product code; review write-up in `docs/reviews/round23-state-machine.md` with the seeds.

Product fixes (`activity_center.py`):
- `_transition` now stamps `started_at` when entering `CANCELLATION_REQUESTED` — without it the snapshot failed `valid_operation` and broke `load_active_operations` round-trips.
- `_record` falls back to `_history_snapshot`, so mutating calls on an evicted-but-resolvable op raise honest `OperationTransitionError` instead of `KeyError`.
- `note_authorities_changed` syncs `_history` rows after in-place snapshot rewrites (`_sync_history_row`), iterates `list(self._records.values())` (the `_transition`→`_archive` path mutates the dict mid-loop → RuntimeError).
- `can_cancel_now` is `False` once `CANCELLATION_REQUESTED` — `request_cancel`/`prepare_shutdown` previously hit the disallowed self-transition.
- `retry` keeps declared `NOT_CANCELLABLE` authoritative: a factory-supplied `cancel_callback` is dropped instead of crashing `submit` on the contradictory pair.

Product fixes (`room_workspace.py`):
- `resolve_dirty_state` precondition guards: `(False, msg)` for `commit_preview`/`cancel_preview` without a preview and `recover_draft`/`discard_recovery` without a candidate — previously reported `(True, …)` for effects that never ran.
- `set_revision_label` passes `label=`/`note=` as keywords — it always raised `TypeError` against the repository's keyword-only signature.

Product fixes (`native_worker.py`):
- `_detach` also releases on `destroyed`: a thread finishing between `thread.wait()` and the `finished.connect` never emitted to the late connection, so a C++-deleted `QThread` stayed in `_LINGERING_THREADS` and `lingering_thread_count()` never drained.
