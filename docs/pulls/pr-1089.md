## Summary

`render_snap_feedback` ignored its `screen_position` argument and pinned every snap label at the renderer's `lower_left`, detaching the snap reason from the point being operated on. This PR moves that readout to a cursor-following Qt HUD and adds typed delta input to the move/rotate gizmo.

**Cursor-side snap HUD (#979):** a `Qt::ToolTip | FramelessWindowHint` `_SnapHud` window (same shape as the #983 pick chooser — never a child of the VTK HWND, `WA_ShowWithoutActivating`, `NoFocus`) anchored at the snap candidate/cursor in interactor DIP space. `render_snap_feedback(label, *, screen_position, hud_lines)` shows it whenever a position is supplied; the `lower_left` `add_text` label stays as the documented fallback when the point cannot be projected. Placement opens right+below the cursor, flips left/above near edges so it never covers the target, and clamps to a 4 px margin inside the interactor — all in DIP, so 200 % DPI and multi-monitor layouts behave. HUD is hidden on interactor Resize/ScreenChangeInternal/Hide.

**Delta + snap-state readout:** during `_preview_move`/`_preview_rotate` the HUD lists the snap line (`頂点 · Front Left (speaker-fl) · X=1.350 m · 6 px` / `グリッドスナップ …` / `角度スナップ …°`), the disabled reason (`スナップ一時解除中 (Shift)`, `…: オフ`, `スナップなし (取得範囲外)`), the `ΔX/ΔY/ΔZ` or `角度` delta in the #496 `LengthDisplayPolicy` unit (internal authority stays metres — `set_length_policy` is bound via `bind_length_policy_widget`), mode state (axis constraint, group count, numeric entry), and the commit hint.

**Numeric delta input:** with a move/rotate gesture armed (M or new `G` alias; R for rotate), typing `0-9`, `.` or `-` opens numeric entry. The typed value is parsed through `display_to_si` and applied as an exact delta through the same `working.begin/preview/commit_preview` port as a pointer drag — Enter commits, Esc cancels without touching `SceneRevision`, Backspace edits, X/Y/Z re-aim the axis live. While entry is active every other key and mouse press is consumed (no Delete/L/arrow misfires), pointer moves cannot overwrite the typed delta, and a click-release commits the typed value rather than the pointer position.

**DPI fix bundled in:** `SnapSelector` radii are DIP values but `_project_to_screen` returned VTK display pixels — at 200 % DPI the effective acquire radius was halved. Projection now goes through the new `world_to_widget_position` (DIP), which also feeds the HUD anchor; unprojectable points park at infinite distance rather than crashing the selector.
