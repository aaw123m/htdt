## Summary

Implements the R160 residual on the typed hybrid broadband composition: **numerical stitching across the wave/GA band boundary**, **evidence-driven automatic crossover selection**, and a **bounded late-energy solver** producing the `LateEnergyDecay` component. All three are audited, bounded, and fail-closed; no production claim is widened.

### New capability

- `htdt/cad_hybrid_stitching.py` — `HybridStitchingPlan` / `StitchedHybridResponseArtifact` compose a `DeterministicPathFrequencyResponseArtifact` (wave lane, exact R130 binding) with an `AggregatedGaComplexResponse` (GA lane) on a declared output grid. Per-output-point `provenance.region` (`wave_only` / `crossover_blend` / `ga_only`) records which lane produced each sample; uncovered intervals are recorded explicitly as `gap_domains` — ambiguous evidence keeps the gap rather than silently joining. Crossover blends use the declared complementary `HybridWeightLaw` (`equal_power_cosine_v1` / `linear_voltage_v1`).
- Automatic crossover selection: `widest_contiguous_agreement_band_v1` sweeps both lanes' complex agreement on the eligible overlap, requires a minimum of 2 samples per band, and fails UNSUPPORTED on ties, insufficient samples, or geometry-domain violations. The selected band is recorded with its full evaluation evidence for audit; explicit crossover configuration remains supported unchanged.
- `htdt/cad_hybrid_late_energy.py` — bounded late-energy solver: derives per-band per-reflection scattering injection from the exact deterministic-path band chain (`scattering_injection_energy` = specular energy × scattering fraction × specular deficit, attenuated by delay), then evaluates `bounded_exponential_after_last_deterministic_arrival_v1` on a declared decay law and emits `LateEnergyDecay` component artifacts (`r160_late_energy_decay_artifacts`) reusable in typed composition.
- Fail-closed matrix: unsupported GA component contributions (diffraction/scattering kinds) poison the GA aggregate to UNSUPPORTED; extraction/extension of input grids and transport/convention mismatches raise `HybridNumericalCompositionError` with `.failure_code`; missing/incomplete deterministic chains, non-positive specular energy, and unbounded decay parameters all fail closed.

### Authority / provenance records

- Native schema v13 (`r160_late_energy_decay_artifacts` + `cad_hybrid_stitching_policies` append-only artifact tables); both audited into the row-integrity unbound-payload ledger and the authority-audit sequence contract.
- `HybridStitchingPolicy` persists mode (`manual` / `automatic`), crossover binding, and provenance so automatic selections stay auditable across save/reopen.
- Follow-up: registers the sibling `cad_late_field_artifacts` (v12) into `_UNBOUND_PAYLOAD_TABLES` so `assert_row_integrity_registry_complete` holds.

### Tests

`backend/tests/test_cad_hybrid_stitching_late_energy.py` (20 tests): stitch plan/grid validation, provenance regions, gap preservation on disagreement, artifact rejection when GA contains unsupported contributions, explicit + automatic crossover selection (including tie → UNSUPPORTED), persisted policy round-trip, late-energy decay generation (values, bounds, band coverage, t≥t_start window), composition reuse, and fail-closed cases. `backend/tests/test_round19_debt_structural.py` fixture generalized for the v13 migration chain.
