## 目的

nominal最適化だけでなく、speaker/seat位置、aim、physical yaw等の現実的な設置誤差・入力不確かさに対して性能がどれだけ安定するかを評価する。

例:

- 案A: nominal FRは最良だが、speaker位置±20 mmで大きく悪化
- 案B: nominalは少し劣るが、同じ±20 mmで性能が安定

HTDTはこれを単一scoreへ潰さず、nominal性能とrobustnessを別objectiveとしてPareto比較する。

## Core authority

- immutable `RobustnessSpec`
- `UncertaintyAxis`
- `PerturbationSample`
- `RobustnessEvaluation`
- exact candidate/SceneRevision/model/objective/sampling/fidelity provenance
- explicit bounded/distribution/empirical/discrete uncertainty semantics
- explicit correlation/linked perturbation model
- no implicit probability distribution from ± tolerance
- finite sample extrema are `sampled_worst`, not unproven `worst_case`

## Initial uncertainty axes

First-line:
- speaker XYZ
- seat/listener XYZ
- acoustic aim yaw/pitch where supported
- physical cabinet yaw / toe-in

Capability-gated later:
- material/boundary uncertainty
- directivity/source sensitivity
- environment
- DSP parameters under a future signal-path authority

## Critical semantics

- O30/O40 objective/Pareto authority is reused, not replaced.
- Every perturbed state reruns applicable G10/O80 hard constraints.
- Infeasible perturbations remain evidence; they are not silently discarded.
- Percentile/probability outputs require an explicit probabilistic uncertainty model.
- O70 residual/model uncertainty and O90 input/tolerance variation remain distinct.
- synthetic lane may validate software behavior but never opens production recommendations.
- production robustness recommendation requires eligible O60/R180 evidence for the model/observable and perturbation domain.
- no opaque overall robustness score.
