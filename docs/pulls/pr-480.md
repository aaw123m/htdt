## Summary

Follow-up fix from the real-display GUI verification of #477: the inspector's 役割 (speaker role) **row label** had no tooltip even though the field did. Cause: `SelectionInspector.__init__` called `self._hint(speaker_form, self.role_field, …)` one statement *before* `speaker_form.addRow("役割", self.role_field)`, so `form.labelForField()` returned `None` and the label assignment silently skipped.

- `room_workspace.py`: moved the `_hint` call after `addRow` — label now carries the role hint like every other row.
- `test_rev32_room_guidance.py`: added `test_inspector_row_labels_carry_the_field_hint` asserting the form row labels (種類/名前/位置/寸法/役割) all carry the field hint — `_hint`-before-`addRow` ordering regressions can't slip through again.

Scoped suite green (11/11 in the guidance file). No other `_hint`-before-`addRow` ordering bug exists — audited all `_hint` call sites.
